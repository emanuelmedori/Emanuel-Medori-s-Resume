Tableau Project

Responding Real-World Questions Through Data Visualization

Questions answered (in order):

  1. What is the average compensation for each job domain?
  2. Considering only companies offering childcare benefits, what are the average compensations by industry for companies with and without 401(k) benefits?
  3. For lower-level positions (intern, entry, and associate), what is the average compensation by city?
  4. What is the popularity (number of followers) of small-medium sized companies (250–1,000 employees), and how does this vary based on how many job postings they’ve made?
  5. What were the total number of full-time job posts and views of those posts by month for the following business domains: Accounting, Administration, Analysis, Business Development, Finance, General Business, Human Resources, Information Technology, Management, Marketing, Product Management, Project Management, Public Relations, Strategy, and Supply Chain?
  6. Using 95% confidence, how many job postings should we expect each month for the next 12 months?
  7. What is the frequency of job postings by country from companies outside of the United States?
  8. What is the quarterly trend in job postings allowing vs disallowing remote work?
